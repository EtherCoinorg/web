What Is Ethercoin?

AI computing power is concentrated in the hands of a few cloud providers. Ethercoin breaks this monopoly.

Ethercoin is a decentralized AI compute network where anyone with a GPU can contribute computing power and earn ETHER. Anyone holding ETHER can access AI inference and optimization services without permission, censorship, or geographic restrictions.

The network measures "useful computation" through a verifiable proof system called EtherFLOP — ensuring every AI operation is real and accurately rewarded. No wasteful mining — every watt powers actual AI workloads.

Beyond compute, Ethercoin hosts the Decentralized Distillation Protocol, a revolutionary system that extracts knowledge from closed-source AI models and releases it as open public goods. Knowledge belongs to humanity.

Core Technology

Every component is designed for verifiability, censorship resistance, and fair incentives.

⚠

Verifiable Compute

EtherFLOP measures useful AI work precisely. A three-layer verification system — optimistic spot checks, zero-knowledge proofs, and TEE attestations — ensures every EFLOP is real.

🔗

Layered Verification

Low-value tasks use fast optimistic verification with random anchor checks. High-value tasks use ZK proofs or TEE remote attestation. Cost scales with task value.

🚪

Anti-Censorship

Onion-routed proxy network distributes API calls across global nodes, making requests untraceable. No single point of blocking or control.

⚡

Distillation Protocol

Extract knowledge from closed-source models through a decentralized network of miners, proxy nodes, and verifiers. Output is CC-licensed open data.

💰

Stake-Based Security

All participants — miners, verifiers, proxy nodes, and challengers — stake ETHER. Dishonest behavior results in slashing, making attacks economically irrational.

📈

On-Chain Reputation

Every node builds a verifiable track record of completed tasks, response times, and accuracy. High-reputation nodes earn better task assignments.

How It Works

From task creation to verified result — a complete lifecycle on the Ethercoin network.

1

Create a Task

Submit a model CID, input data, and expected EFLOPs. Lock ETHER as payment and challenge stake. Choose verification mode: optimistic, ZK, or TEE.

2

Match & Execute

The decentralized scheduler matches your task to the best GPU node based on reputation, latency, and stake. The node runs the inference or optimization workload.

3

Verify & Settle

The node submits a WorkProof with anchor hashes or ZK proof. Verifiers check correctness during the challenge period. Rewards are released upon verification.

4

Distill & Open

For distillation tasks, verified data points pass similarity checks and are published as open-source datasets. Knowledge flows freely to everyone.

ETHER is the native currency of the Ethercoin network. 90% of the 21 trillion supply is minted at genesis for early contributors, development, and community building. The remaining 10% is released over 10 years (1% annually) through verifiable compute and distillation mining, with rewards tied to EtherFLOP contribution. After mining ends, the community may vote to activate a 0.1% perpetual annual incentive. Combined with regular burning of fees and penalties, this creates a potentially deflationary value mechanism for long-term holders.

FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

What makes Ethercoin different from other AI crypto projects?

Ethercoin focuses on verifiable useful compute — every EFLOP is measured and verified through a layered proof system, not self-reported. The distillation protocol is a unique application that turns closed-source knowledge into open public goods.

How is compute verified without trusted third parties?

Three verification modes: optimistic anchor checks (random layer activations), zero-knowledge proofs (zk-SNARKs), and TEE remote attestations. Each mode is suited to different task values and privacy requirements.

Can anyone participate in the distillation protocol?

Yes. GPU miners process distillation tasks. Proxy operators share API access through an onion-routed network. Verifiers validate submitted data. All participants stake ETHER and earn rewards.

When will the mainnet launch?

Phase 1 (Genesis) launches in 2026 Q3–Q4, supporting inference tasks, mining, and the distillation protocol beta. See the roadmap in the whitepaper for details.
